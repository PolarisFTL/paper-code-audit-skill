评分如何计算How the score is calculated

条目得分：一致 1、部分一致 0.5、不一致 0、未知 0。未知保留在分母中；只有具备理由和证据的不适用（NA）条目才从分母排除。每个类别内部的有效条目等权。Item values: matched 1, partial 0.5, mismatched 0, unknown 0. Unknowns remain in the denominator. Only justified, evidenced not-applicable (NA) items are excluded. Applicable items have equal weight within each category.

类别一致性 = 100 × 条目得分之和 ÷ 有效条目数
总分 = Σ（有效类别权重 × 类别一致性）÷ Σ 有效类别权重Category consistency = 100 × sum of item values ÷ applicable item count
Overall score = Σ (active category weight × category consistency) ÷ Σ active category weights

六类基础权重依次为 25 / 25 / 15 / 15 / 10 / 10。全为 NA 的类别不参与加权，其余类别权重归一化。类别覆盖率 = 已判定条目数 ÷ 有效条目数，总覆盖率按同一类别权重加权；未知上限仅将未知项暂按 1 计算，不改变其他状态。Base category weights are 25 / 25 / 15 / 15 / 10 / 10 in table order. Entirely NA categories are excluded and remaining weights are normalized. Category coverage = assessed items ÷ applicable items; overall coverage uses the same category weights. The unknown upper bound changes only unknown item values to 1.

总分四舍五入至两位小数后分档。分档是静态检查的解释规则，未经校准为复现成功概率；关键缺失或错误仍须单独阅读。Grades use the overall score rounded to two decimals. These thresholds interpret a static audit and are not calibrated reproduction probabilities. Read critical gaps or errors separately.

04检查边界与待核实事项Limits & open questions

如何理解这份报告How to read this report

静态一致性只能反映本次可获取材料中的对应关系。它不能单独证明训练收敛、指标达标、环境可安装或完整实验可运行。未知意味着证据不足；“代码存在”也不自动证明该代码在目标流程中被调用。Static consistency describes relationships in the materials available for this audit. It alone cannot establish training convergence, reported metrics, environment installability, or an executable full experiment. Unknown means insufficient evidence; existing code does not automatically prove use in the target pipeline.
